About Us

Leading manufacturer of disposable products

Manufacturing is carried out to set industry norms and guidelines, using the finest raw materials and modern machines to keep a consistent standard of quality across the range.

Morooj Nizwa International Company LLC, established in 2009, is a regional leader providing aluminium foils, aluminium and plastic containers, clingfilm and other plastic wraps, paper, plastic and foam cups, paper, plastic and foam plates, tableware, paper and plastic bags, hygiene and safety products, detergents and customized products for each market segment, with manufacturing and distribution facilities throughout Oman.

Our vision. To keep rising in the business, constantly upgrading systems and technology to keep pace with advancing times, while keeping customers satisfied on delivery time and cost.

Our mission. To become the number one provider of complete disposable food packing solutions, in quality, hygienic and safe products that offer customer delight.
