# Monipack Redesign Showcase

A modern, responsive web application and redesign showcase built for **Morooj Nizwa International Company LLC (Monipack)** — a regional manufacturer and distributor of disposable food packaging based in Nizwa, Oman.

**Live URL:** [https://demomonipack.netlify.app/](https://demomonipack.netlify.app/)
**Og Website:** [https://www.monipack.com/](https://monipack.com/)

## Overview

This website serves as a redesign concept and client showcase for Monipack, established in 2009. It highlights high-quality disposable food packaging solutions across paper, plastic, aluminium, and foam lines while providing practical, interactive self-service tools for B2B clients and registered accounts.

---

## Key Features & Interactive Tools

The platform is designed with user experience and utility in mind, featuring specialized self-service modules:

1. **Quick Order Upload (CSV)**
* Allows business clients to upload purchase order spreadsheets directly from their browser.
* *Privacy-first:* Rows are processed entirely on the client side; no sensitive data is transmitted or stored externally.


2. **Instant Quote Builder**
* Configurable selectors for material type, size, and quantity.
* Generates an instant quote summary that can be copied or printed as a PDF.


3. **Volume Pricing Calculator**
* Dynamically calculates tier-based discounts based on case counts:
* **1 to 4 cases:** List price
* **5 to 19 cases:** 10% discount
* **20+ cases:** 18% discount




4. **Comprehensive Portfolio & Industry Targeting**
* Showcases product lines spanning paper, plastic, aluminium, and foam items.
* Highlights industries served, including hotels, hospitals, food chains, catering, supermarkets, airlines, and government sectors.
